\noindent Assume $A, B$ are ``generic'' $n \times n$ matrices, $T$ is a triangular $n \times n$ matrix, and $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{x}, \mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^n$. In terms of floating point operations, determine the computational complexity of each of the ``operations'' below. Give your answer in terms of ``big O'', e.g.\ $O(n^p)$ for some $p$. For credit, you must answer \textit{all} of the below correctly. \par\vspace{0.3cm}

% Parts
\begin{enumerate}[(a)]
\item \rule{1in}{0.4pt} : \quad Dot Products, $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}$. \vspace{2em}

\item \rule{1in}{0.4pt} : \quad Matrix-vector multiplication, $A\mathbf{v}$ \vspace{2em}

\item \rule{1in}{0.4pt} : \quad Matrix-matrix multiplication, $AB$ \vspace{2em}

\item \rule{1in}{0.4pt} : \quad Computing the RREF of $A$ \vspace{2em}

\item \rule{1in}{0.4pt} : \quad Computing $\det A$ with cofactor expansion. \vspace{2em}

\item \rule{1in}{0.4pt} : \quad Solving $T\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ with ``back/foward substitution'' \vspace{2em}
\end{enumerate}
